## Airwindows ChimeyGuitar, ChimeyGuitar2 and ChimeyDeluxe

Copyright (c) Chris Johnson / Airwindows, MIT license. The full notice is in `third_party/airwindows/LICENSE` and retained in each source file.

Source: https://github.com/airwindows/airwindows/tree/7fd5f5cae262a41e6dd7d9a83d60c46e84d3092a/plugins/WinVST

The three corresponding directories contain unmodified upstream `.h`, `.cpp`, and `Proc.cpp` files, retrieved November 6, 2026. They are compiled with a minimal independently written VST2 compatibility shim for reference tests only. No Steinberg VST2 SDK is included or required.

`Source/*Engine.*` are reproducible SDK-free ports generated by `scripts/port-engines.py`. The port retains the original float/double DSP expressions, initialization, and defaults. Integration changes: deterministic seeds instead of `rand()`, a sample-rate member, a reset method, and a finite/clamped parameter setter. The wrapper adds model routing, UI/state, mono handling, peak meters, and dry comparison. It adds no loudness compensation.
